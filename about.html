About

Hello, welcome to The Secure Planet. This page provides a high-level overview about this website & its owner. I have personally coded & designed this website.

Who am I?

I started from scratch learning basics of systems/network & playing with Python/Perl. I did not jump to this career because it's considered cool & in high demand now. I started my career in the world of Information Security in the 1990s due to pure passion on this subject. This passion keeps me kicking.

Jobs die, passion don't :)

I am a geek at heart & pure techie by mind who has hands-on experience on the following domains & technologies. I personally believe hackers & security professionals are two sides of the same coin — it just depends on the "motive" of the click, after all everyone uses a keyboard & mouse.

Thought Leadership — CxO Board Risk Management Corporate Security Consulting Cyber Security, Intelligence & Cyber Warfare Corporate Governance & Legal Regulatory Compliance Computer Forensics & Cyber Crime Prevention Audit & Compliance — ISO27002, GDPR, PCI DSS, HIPAA, GLBA, FISAP, FSA/FCA, FISMA, OCC, PIPEDA, BASEL Vulnerability Assessment & Penetration Testing Bug Bounties Product Security & IIoT
