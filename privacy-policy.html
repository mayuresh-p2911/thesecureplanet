PRIVACY POLICY

Your data,
handled responsibly

This privacy policy outlines the types of personal information which is received and collected by TheSecurePlanet.com. Log files record IP addresses, browser type, ISP, timestamps, referring/exit pages & click counts to analyze trends — never linked to anything personally identifiable.

Subscriber of Our Email Newsletter

Privacy of TheSecurePlanet.com website visitors is extremely important. We are constantly striving to ensure that no spam is sent from our email servers. TheSecurePlanet.com manages advanced automated systems as well as human verification processes in order to take immediate action whenever we see a potential risk of irrelevant or undesired content. We respect your privacy and take protecting it seriously.

Cookies

Our site may use cookies to enhance the experience of the users. Users may also set their browser to stop storage of cookies, or to alert you when the cookies are being sent, and if they do so note that some part of our site may fail to function properly or in its best way. Some of the ads may be served by Google.com. Google's use of the DART cookie enables it to serve ads to users based on their visit to our site. DART doesn't use any personal identification information about you. If you wish to disable cookies, you may do so through your individual browser options. More detailed information about cookie management with specific web browsers can be found on the browsers' respective websites.

We do not tolerate spam and will never sell, rent, lease or give away your information (name, email, etc.). You will only receive the emails that you permitted upon subscription. If you post any personally identifiable information in comments below TheSecurePlanet.com posts, then this may be displayed publicly. If you require any more information or have any questions about our privacy policy, please feel free to contact us.

Fair Use Guide of TheSecurePlanet.com

Always provide a hyperlink (full URL) to the TheSecurePlanet.com article where the quote is from, and do not use coding techniques (like rel=nofollow) that try to hide credits from search engine bots.

You may not use an IFRAME tag when linking to content on TheSecurePlanet.com, as that will hide the original URL referral.

TheSecurePlanet.com takes plagiarism very seriously. If we discover a website or blog republishing our RSS feeds or website content illegally, we will file a formal DMCA complaint with their advertising partners (like Google AdSense) and the abuse department of their web hosting company & ISP.

This privacy policy is subject to change without notice. You are requested to visit this page for the latest privacy policy of www.TheSecurePlanet.com.
